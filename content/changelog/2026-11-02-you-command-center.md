The You tab now has four folders: **Review requested**, **Opened**, **Merged** and **Snoozed**. **Opened** lists all your open PRs in one place, grouped by what each one needs: held in the queue, changes requested, checks failing, open review comments, ready to queue, in the queue, waiting on others, and drafts. What needs you comes first, and its count shows in red next to the folder.

You can act from the list itself: **Queue**, **Dequeue** or **Publish** sits on each row, so you don't have to open the PR first.

A PR whose checks are still running, or that waits on a review, now shows as **Ready to queue** in repos Mergepatrol runs on. The queue waits for both on its own, so queueing it is your only move. It used to sit in Waiting on others.
