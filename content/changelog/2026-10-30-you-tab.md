**You** gathers your open PRs from every repo, and the PRs that ask for your review or your
team's, in one place. Your PRs are sorted by whose move it is: your turn, in the queue, waiting on
others, drafts, and what merged this week.

Approve, request changes or comment without leaving the tab. Each review waits five seconds with
**Undo** before it goes to GitHub, since GitHub can't take one back.

![A PR held in the queue until it gets a review](/changelog/you-tab/held.png)

When a PR waits on reviewers who haven't answered, **Nudge** comments with an @mention of each.

![The Nudge button on a PR waiting on others](/changelog/you-tab/nudge.png)
