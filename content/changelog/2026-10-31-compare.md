The new [Compare](/compare) pages put Mergepatrol next to GitHub's merge queue, Mergify, Graphite,
Aviator and Trunk: what each costs, what your CI has to change, and what happens to a PR that
conflicts or fails its checks. Each page also says where the other queue is the better fit.

The blog has two new articles: [What is a merge queue](/blog/what-is-a-merge-queue), and [How to
merge stacked pull requests](/blog/merge-stacked-pull-requests).
