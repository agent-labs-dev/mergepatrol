The **Queue** page now always shows **Merge lane**, **Getting ready**, **Held**, **Ejected** and **Open**, so the page keeps its shape as PRs move. A list with no PRs says so.

A PR you queue now starts in **Getting ready**, and no longer shows in the merge lane first.
