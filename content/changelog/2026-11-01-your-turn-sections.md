title: Your turn splits your PRs by what they need
kind: improved
image: /changelog/your-turn-sections/after.png
---

**Your turn** on the You tab now sorts your PRs into sections: held in the queue and needing your decision, ready to queue, changes requested, checks failing, and not queued because Mergepatrol is off in the repo. You can see what to queue and what needs you at a glance. Empty sections don't show.
