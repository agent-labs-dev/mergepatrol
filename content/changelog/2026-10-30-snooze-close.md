The clock button snoozes a PR until later today, tomorrow, next week or in a month. It comes
back sooner if someone else comments or reviews. Press `s` to snooze the PR you're on.

Snoozed PRs wait in their own **Snoozed** folder, where **Unsnooze** brings one back early.

![The Snoozed folder](/changelog/snooze-close/snoozed.png)

The close button beside it closes a PR that went stale, such as an old dependency bump, as you
on GitHub. Like a review, it waits five seconds with **Undo** first.

**Approve** and **Approve & queue** are now one button, like GitHub's merge button. Pick one from
its menu and it does it, and the button keeps doing that on the next PR until you pick the other.
