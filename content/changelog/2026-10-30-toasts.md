---
title: Clearer notifications that stack
kind: improved
image: /changelog/toasts/stack.png
---

Notifications now stack in the bottom right corner instead of piling up in two places. Hover the
stack to see all of them, or swipe one away.

A review or a close from **You** shows a ring that runs down while you can still **Undo**. When it
goes to GitHub, the same notification changes to say it's done.
